LOUISVILLE Barnes & Noble to open new Louisville-area store. Here's when it'll open Caroline Neal Louisville Courier Journal May 18, 2026, 12:16 p.m. ET Barnes & Noble is opening a new store at Mall St. Matthews in 2026, officials confirmed to The Courier Journal. The new store will be located in Suite 1493, which previously housed Zappos Unboxed, Vice President of Store Design Janine Flanigan said in a statement. According to Barnes & Noble , the store is expected to open in October. “We are thrilled to open another Barnes & Noble in Louisville and to bring even more access to books and a welcoming bookstore experience to the community,” Flanigan said. “Louisville has been a wonderful market for us, and this new location at Mall St. Matthews gives us the opportunity to serve even more readers, families, students and customers across the area.” Drawings for the space, which will be more than 20,000 square feet, are currently being completed. A contractor for the project has not yet been selected, and Flanigan declined to share the estimated cost. In addition to books, gifts, toys and games, the Mall St. Matthews store will have a Barnes & Noble Café and “inviting spaces for customers to browse, gather and discover their next great read,” Flanigan said. Barnes & Noble has two Jefferson County stores at the Shops at Paddock and the Shoppes at Plainview as well as a Hardin County store at Elizabethtown Mall . Flanigan confirmed that there are no plans to close the existing locations. The announcement comes after the last Louisville-area Zappos store at Mall St. Matthews permanently closed. As of March 19, the signage at the store had been removed from the exterior, and the interior was undergoing an active demolition. The Zappos Unboxed at Mall St. Matthews first opened in April 2021 .
