Vancouver's location was announced last year, at the corner of Granville and Smithe in the former Cinema Public House space. A sign up at the incoming location doesn't give the days, but does note the store will be open from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. What's on the menu at Dave's Hot Chicken Dave's has a simple menu focused on hot chicken tenders and sliders, which are offered in spice levels ranging from “No Spice” to “Reaper." The casual restaurant also offers sides like a kale slaw, mac and cheese, and regular or cheese-topped fries. With files from Lindsay William Ross.
