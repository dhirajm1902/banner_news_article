Ng said Tomei expects to maintain a near-flat network expansion, with any new store openings largely offset by closures of weaker outlets. He said the cautious expansion strategy comes as the group anticipates business momentum to moderate in the coming quarters, following a peak festive period and amid elevated gold prices that have affected affordability and demand. Ng noted that gold prices have remained volatile this year due to geopolitical tensions, rising sharply before correcting from recent highs. Despite the fluctuations, he said Tomei's profitability remains relatively stable due to its mark-to-market pricing approach, where retail prices are adjusted in line with prevailing gold prices. "When the price drops, we reduce prices accordingly, and when prices rise, we adjust according to the market," he said.
