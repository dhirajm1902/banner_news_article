Here's a look at the latest grocery store openings and food retail projects across Delaware. Lidl to move into former Rite Aid site Discount grocer Lidl plans to open a store at the Shoppes of Graylyn in Brandywine Hundred in late 2026, taking over the space formerly occupied by Rite Aid at 1732 Marsh Road. At 21,300 square feet, it is the largest retail space in the shopping center. Lidl’s model centers on a streamlined shopping experience, with roughly 80% of its products sold under private labels. The company says limiting brand selection allows it to source high-quality goods while keeping prices low. Founded in Germany, Lidl now operates in 31 countries. The company established its U.S. headquarters in Arlington, Virginia, in 2015 and has opened 192 stores across 10 states and Washington, DC, according to data company ScrapeHero . Lidl operates three stores in Delaware, located in Dover, Middletown and Bear. Redner’s Market expands with food-hall concept in Milltown Pennsylvania-based Redner’s Market is also expanding its Delaware footprint, with a grocery store planned for the Kirkwood Plaza Shopping Center in Milltown.
