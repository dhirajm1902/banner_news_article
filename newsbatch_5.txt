Sales at other hardware stores may not have been adequate, leading to store closures. True Value Hardware cooperative Rooster Home & Hardware is closing at the end of June. Shutterstock Rooster Home & Hardware closes shop Award-winning cooperative chain store Rooster Home & Hardware True Value in Texas said it will close its popular shop on June 30 after holding a store closing sale. The Dallas-based, family-owned True Value Hardware store is advertising its Store Closing Sale, offering 50% off on everything through May 31 at its 10233 E. Northwest Highway, No. 409, location. The store's owners did not state a reason for closing the shop, according to a May Facebook announcement. It did not file for bankruptcy. The store, which opened in 2016, has not said what discounts will be offered in its final month. The hardware store launched a 25%-off-everything sale from May 1 through May 14, but did not call it a closing sale. It increased the discount to 50% on May 15 for the announced closing sale.
