Burger House was my go to spot both when I was in high school in College Station , and when I ended up at Texas A&M . The restaurant is housed in a dumpy, weird-looking house with various pipes and AC-ducts haphazardly attached to it. The inside was similarly unassuming, with its brick floor tiling and various local newspaper reviews tacked to the walls here and there. There was a slight Korean flair, reflecting the family who founded it. The chairs felt like they were repurposed from a '90s-era A&M classroom. The inside of Burger House in Bryan, Texas. TripAdvisor
