(Image provided by city of Beloit) By Kevin Haas Rock River Current Get our free e-newsletter BELOIT, Wis. — The Ho-Chunk Nation has announced a grand opening date for its new casino near Willowbrook and Colley roads, just off Interstate 90. Ho-Chunk Gaming Beloit, 777 Willowbrook Road, will open to the public at 2 p.m. Friday, Oct. 23. It will then operate 24 hours a day and seven days a week. “We are proud to welcome guests to Ho-Chunk Gaming Beloit and begin this new chapter in our community. This opening reflects our commitment to creating a premier entertainment destination while contributing to local economic growth and providing meaningful opportunities for the Beloit area,” Steven Abangan, executive manager of Ho-Chunk Gaming Beloit, said in a news release. “We look forward to serving our guests and becoming a valued part of the community.” The casino will have approximately 1,300 slot machines and 40 table games, including blackjack, Texas hold’em, 3-card poker, Mississippi stud and ultimate Texas hold’em. The property also includes Draft Picks Pub House, Black River Coffee Shop, Food Truck Alley and a parking garage. A second phase of the project includes an 18-floor hotel with 312 guest rooms and a 2,000-seat conference center, which are both slated to open in fall 2027. The hotel will include a rooftop lounge, lobby bar, pool and spa. The venue was designed by HBG Design and Corporate Contractors Inc. is the general contractor. Once the casino and hotel are operational, Ho-Chunk is expected to be Beloit’s largest employer with roughly 1,500 workers.
