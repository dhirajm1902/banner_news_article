Red River Co-op to open grocery store, pharmacy in redeveloped Portage Place Red River Co-op will open a full-service grocery store and pharmacy as part of the redevelopment of the former Portage Place mall in downtown Winnipeg. Sofia Frolova reports. By News Staff Posted June 24, 2026 1:57 pm. Last Updated June 24, 2026 7:29 pm. Red River Co-op will open a full-service grocery store and pharmacy as part of the redevelopment of the former Portage Place mall in downtown Winnipeg. The cooperative announced Wednesday it plans to open a 22,500-square-foot food store and a 6,500-square-foot pharmacy in 2029, providing residents with access to fresh food, household essentials and health services. “Red River Co-op was founded nearly 90 years ago on the belief that people working together can create stronger communities. Our investment in the Portage Place redevelopment reflects that same belief today,” said Craig Gilpin, Red River Co-op CEO. “This project is about more than building a food store and pharmacy, it is about bringing essential services back to the heart of our city, investing locally, and helping create a downtown Winnipeg that future generations will be proud to call their own.” The new location will be Red River Co-op’s 10th food store and pharmacy and is expected to create 57 jobs. The store will be a key component of the Portage Place redevelopment project, which also includes a Healthcare Centre of Excellence and a residential tower. Project officials say access to a full-service grocery store has long been identified as a priority for downtown residents as Winnipeg’s downtown population continues to grow.
