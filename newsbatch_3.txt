Title: Rulli Brothers now open on Sundays
Status hint: Opening | Industry: Specialty / Other
Published: 2026-05-24 12:43:51

Lorie Barber Fri, May 22, 2026 at 3:32 PM UTC 0 (WKBN) – A big change is coming for Rulli Brothers shoppers. The grocery stores will now be open on Sundays. “I feel like that will be really helpful for me and the community, that we actually have somewhere to shop on Sundays now,” said Shahni Ali, a customer. For 110 years, Rulli’s was closed on Sundays, treating it as a day of rest and prayer. Now for the first time, starting this Sunday, the stores will be open. “We’ve been thinking about it for quite some time,” owner Mark Rulli said. “I think it’s been trending in that direction for quite a while. People need to get what they need when they need it, and we’re trying to provide as much convenience as possible for the customers.” Rulli said the change was needed as data now shows more families are using Sundays as a day to prepare for the week ahead. While this may adjust employee schedules, Rulli says the store has hired more people to work those Sunday shifts to bridge the gap. “I think it’s going to be positive. Our current employees are going to get more hours, so I think it’s going to be positive all the way around,” he said. Office Manager Stephanie Tomko agrees. Advertisement “I think it’s going to be an overall good thing. They’re going to hire enough people that they’re going to make it work out for everybody, so I think the ones who want to have it off, hopefully, will be able to keep that day off,” she said. The extra hours will also allow the store to open its platform to deliveries through Instacart. While the stores will still be closed on major holidays, Rulli says he believes this will be a positive change for everyone. “People have been asking for quite a while. I mean, people have got to go to the store on Sundays and get stuff. They run out of something.
