Hyderabad : Mangalya Shopping Mall inaugurated its 32nd branch at Dilsukhnagar, Hyderabad, on Friday. Popular actress Meenakshi Chaudhary attended as the chief guest and inaugurated the new showroom amid enthusiastic crowds. Speaking on the occasion, Meenakshi Chaudhary expressed confidence that the people of Dilsukhnagar would support the new store and contribute to its success. Directors Pulluru Narasimha Murthy, Kasam Namashivaya, Kasam Mallikarjun, Kasam Kedarinath, Kasam Shiva Prasad and Pulluru Arun Kumar said the brand continues to grow by offering quality apparel at affordable prices while blending traditional handloom products with modern fashion trends. The new outlet features a wide range of fashion collections for men, women and children, including exclusive wedding and festive wear. The management announced plans to expand further across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu in the coming years. The directors invited customers and families to visit the new store and experience a complete family shopping destination under one roof. Title: Vecina to Open Second Location in Scottsdale This Summer
Status hint: Opening | Industry: Specialty / Other
Published: 2026-07-18 01:14:25

Vecina logo (Image credit: Vecina) SHARE Vecina , the chef-driven Latin-inspired restaurant in Phoenix’s Arcadia neighborhood, is expanding to Scottsdale. According to the Phoenix New Times , the restaurant is expected to open its second location in late August at 8300 Hayden Road, Suite D-101 , in McCormick Ranch, taking over the former Z’Tejas restaurant. Owned by husband-and-wife team James and Raenee Fox , Vecina opened its original Phoenix location in 2019 and was named a James Beard Award semifinalist for Best New Restaurant in 2020. The Scottsdale restaurant will bring the same modern American menu inspired by Latin flavors that has made the original location a local favorite. Guests can expect a rotating selection of shareable dishes featuring Latin-inspired spices, chiles and ingredients, alongside craft cocktails, wine and beer. Current menu offerings at the Arcadia restaurant include hamachi ceviche, carnitas empanadas, mesquite grilled carne asada ribeye, pork belly tacos, skirt steak tacos and elote, with the menu rotating throughout the year based on seasonal ingredients. The larger Scottsdale space will allow the restaurant to expand beyond its current dinner-only service. Plans call for lunch, brunch, happy hour, private dining, additional wood-fired and raw menu offerings, and a lakeside patio for guests. The new restaurant will also feature design elements familiar to fans of the Arcadia location, including a mural by Arizona artist Tato Caraveo, wood accents, community tables, and a glass-enclosed wine room. Additional details about the Scottsdale location are expected to be shared on Vecina’s Instagram page ahead of the opening.
