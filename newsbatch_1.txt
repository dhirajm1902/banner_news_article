The former are available throughout the store, including positions in sushi, pizza, restaurant foods, deli, cheese, seafood and bakery. Part‑time jobs, including cashiers, are also open. Those interested may apply online at jobs.wegmans.com/ballantyne or call 980‑467‑7333 for more information. Wegmans offers benefits to both full‑ and part‑time employees, including flexible scheduling, career development opportunities and tuition assistance through its employee scholarship program. The Ballantyne store will feature traditional Wegmans departments, including restaurant foods (sushi, pizza, chef‑made salads, sandwiches), a produce department with hundreds of fruits and vegetables, and a wide variety of seafood, meat, bakery, deli and cheese options. The store also will offer a large selection of wine and beer. Wegmans has been named one of the “100 Best Companies to Work For” by Fortune for 29 consecutive years, ranking fifth in 2026. The family‑owned chain operates 114 stores along the East Coast and celebrated its 100th anniversary in 2016.
